The problem is not a lack of study material. The problem is having the right questions, in the right format, at the right stage of preparation.

PROBLEM 01

We tend to postpone answer writing

Answer writing often gets pushed aside because we do not have properly curated questions relevant to our stage of preparation. Instead, we remain dependent on external frameworks and wait for the "right" time to start practicing.

PROBLEM 02

We do not have an easy QCAB format

Practicing questions should be simple and repeatable. But without a convenient QCAB format, creating a question set and getting into the habit of writing answers becomes unnecessarily difficult.

PROBLEM 03

Preparation is fragmented away from PYQs

PYQs, answer-writing practice and UPSC-format answer pages often remain in separate silos. The most relevant questions — actual UPSC PYQs — are therefore not naturally integrated into our regular answer writing practice.
